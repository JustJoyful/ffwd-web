## Note on Meridian's licence
- Netflix Open Content states its content is under CC BY 4.0. The owner reviewed the CC BY 4.0 text and decided to use Meridian on that basis (22 Nov 2026).
- The Xiph mirror of the Meridian IMF package ships a CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licence file. We do not use that copy.
- CC BY 4.0 requires: credit to the creator, a link to the licence, and a note that the material was modified. The page credit does all three.
